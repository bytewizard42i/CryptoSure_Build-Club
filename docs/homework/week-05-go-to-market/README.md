# Week 5 — Go-To-Market: First 100 Users

**Who we're after first:** people and small companies who hold meaningful
crypto in self-custody and already know they should be worried about it.
They don't need to be taught that wallets get drained — they need to be
told insurance finally exists for it.

## The two channels

| Channel | Why this one | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Midnight community (Discord, ambassadors, Build Club)** | I've been in the Midnight ecosystem since before Build Club or NIGHTFORCE existed — I'm an ambassador with established trust here, not a newcomer asking for attention. These users already understand zero-knowledge proofs, so "insurance that never sees your identity" lands in one sentence instead of needing a whitepaper. First 100 users should be people who get it immediately. | ~$0. Time only — a few hours a week in Discord, one demo at a community call. |
| **2. Self-custody wallet & custody partners** | They already have exactly our users. A wallet provider that can say "you can insure what's on this device" gets a feature; we get distribution to users at the moment they're thinking about protecting what they hold. | ~$0 cash. Integration/BD time, plus a referral share of premium if a deal lands — deferred cost, not upfront. |

## Why not the others

- **Paid ads** — insurance is a trust product. Nobody buys coverage from an ad; they buy it because someone they trust said it's real.
- **Broad X/Twitter push** — useful for listening (our poll told us what people value), wrong tool for first customers. Cold reach converts badly when the ask is "trust me with your wallet."
- **Conferences / PR** — too slow for a hundred users, and we don't have a sellable product for a press cycle yet. Pitch day is the PR moment, not before.

## The actual first messages

**Message 1 — Midnight Discord:**

> Hey folks — building something at Build Club I'd genuinely like your eyes
> on. It's insurance for crypto in self-custody wallets, and the part I
> think you'll appreciate: underwriting works off ZK proofs, so a carrier
> can verify you qualify *without ever holding your identity or knowing
> your balance.* Coverage for verified wallet compromise. I'm looking for
> ~25 people willing to kick the tires on an early demo and tell me what's
> broken. Who's in?

**Message 2 — wallet provider / custody partner BD email:**

> Subject: a feature your users have been asking for
>
> Hi [name] — I run CryptoSure. We're building insurance for assets held
> in self-custody: your users prove control of their wallet with a ZK
> proof, we underwrite against verifiable criteria, and nobody's identity
> or balance ever leaves their device.
>
> For you it's a retention feature — "this wallet can be insured" — with
> zero custody changes on your side. We're onboarding our first 100 users
> now and I'd like [wallet] to be one of the two partners we launch with.
> 20 minutes to show you the binding flow?

## If a community already has our users — named

- **Midnight Discord + ambassador network** — warm, privacy-native, free
- **Build Club cohort + director network** — reviewers who become amplifiers
- **Lace / Midnight wallet user base** — the natural first integration target
- **Self-custody communities** (hardware wallet forums) — second wave, reached *through* partners rather than directly

*The line that matters: first hundred users come from rooms where "your
keys, your proof, our risk" needs no explaining.*
