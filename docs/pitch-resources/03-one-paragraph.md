# One paragraph

CryptoSure is privacy-preserving insurance for crypto held in wallets, built on Midnight. We cover two things: a wallet over time, and a single high-value transfer at the moment it happens, which is where most money is lost. Our thesis is that crypto is the most insurable asset class ever created, because every loss leaves a permanent trail and ownership can be proven cryptographically, yet nobody has built the insurance market for it. The hard part is that the people who most need coverage are the least willing to hand an insurer their identity, address, and wallet. So we do not ask. A trusted issuer verifies the customer, seals the result, and holds the key; CryptoSure receives only a proof that the wallet is clean, the holder is verified, and they live where we are licensed to sell. We can show a carrier that every insured wallet traces to a real person through lawful process, while holding nothing ourselves that could leak. We are going carrier-backed, Pennsylvania first, business customers first, and we will do one thing well: insure crypto in wallets, honestly.
